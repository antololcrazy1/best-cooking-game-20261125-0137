SIZZLE & SERVE — Rush-Hour Kitchen

How to play: move with WASD / arrow keys or click a station to walk there. Press E (or click the station) to use it. Grab ingredients at the fridge (keys 1–5), chop tomato & lettuce on the board, grill patties, build the dish on the plating counter, serve it at the window before the ticket runs out. P pauses, M mutes.
